# Reference Processes

The current GAPS profile is validated against four reference processes. They are intentionally different so the model is not shaped around one workflow.

## Software delivery

Stress area: requirements, technical design, implementation, verification, closure, and generated skill-package binding.

[View process spec](https://github.com/awjreynolds/governed-autonomy/blob/main/gaps/examples/gadd/ga-process.yml)

## Compliance review casework

Stress area: adaptive case flow, long-running state, statutory or policy deadlines, named identities, multiple authority levels, budget or resource gates, and event-driven escalation.

[View process spec](https://github.com/awjreynolds/governed-autonomy/blob/main/gaps/examples/compliance-review/ga-process.yml)

## Incident response

Stress area: severity escalation, time-boxed containment, rollback authority, evidence preservation, communication approval, and closure under operational pressure.

[View process spec](https://github.com/awjreynolds/governed-autonomy/blob/main/gaps/examples/incident-response/ga-process.yml)

## Procurement approval

Stress area: budget gates, supplier-risk review, segregation of duties, contract handoff, conflict escalation, and renewal monitoring.

[View process spec](https://github.com/awjreynolds/governed-autonomy/blob/main/gaps/examples/procurement-approval/ga-process.yml)

## Validation claim

Validation means the examples conform to this repository's current exploratory GAPS profile and semantic checks. It is not regulatory compliance, certification, proof of executable correctness, legal sufficiency, or a BPMN/CMMN/DMN/OSCAL export.
